Common questions

Before you begin.

How much does an interior design project cost?

Every project is different. The final investment depends on property size, scope, materials, custom furniture and execution requirements. We begin with a consultation and prepare a project-specific proposal.

Do you provide complete turnkey interior solutions?

Yes. We can handle space planning, drawings, 3D visualisation, material selection, civil work, custom furniture, modular solutions, site supervision and final handover.

Do you offer a free initial consultation?

Yes. You can begin with a complimentary introductory call or site consultation to discuss your requirements and understand the next steps.

Which areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Mulund and nearby areas across Mumbai and MMR, including Thane and Navi Mumbai. Selected projects are undertaken across Maharashtra.

Can you design only a kitchen or one room?

Yes. Depending on scope and availability, we undertake focused kitchen, room and renovation assignments as well as complete-property projects.

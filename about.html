About Round Colour Designs

A shared love for design became a shared studio.

Founded in 2022 by Akshay and Pratibha Vanare, Round Colour Designs is built on complementary skills, personal involvement and a belief that every space deserves thoughtful attention.

How it began

Akshay and Pratibha first met while working in the interior design industry. Their professional collaboration grew into friendship, love and eventually a partnership founded on a shared enthusiasm for creating meaningful spaces.

Pratibha’s strength in understanding clients and planning functional kitchens complemented Akshay’s experience in project design, technical coordination and site execution. They enjoyed working together so much that starting their own studio became a natural next step.

Today, they bring that same collaborative energy to every project, approaching each home and workspace with the care they would bring to designing for themselves.

Personal attention is not an add-on. It is how the studio works.

Clients work directly with the founders through the major decisions of the project.
